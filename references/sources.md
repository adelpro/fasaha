# Source

**QALB Guidelines v0.90** — Wajdi Zaghouani, Nizar Habash, Behrang Mohit.
Carnegie Mellon University Qatar & Columbia University, December 2013.

Direct link: http://nlp.qatar.cmu.edu/qalb/QALB-guidelines_0.90.pdf

The reference annotation guidelines for the QALB corpus — 2 million words of Arabic text
(native speaker, non-native speaker, and machine-translation output) manually corrected by
human annotators. Defines the six error categories used in this skill (spelling, punctuation,
lexical, morphology, syntax, dialect), gives 50+ worked correction examples, and includes a
full reference summary of Arabic spelling, punctuation, and number rules. Section 3.2
specifically addresses MT-output correction as distinct from human-text correction, which is
why this is the anchor source for the skill.

`references/qalb-spelling-rules.md` and `references/mt-examples.md` in this skill are both
condensed directly from this document.
